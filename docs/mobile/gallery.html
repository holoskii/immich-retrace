Retrace mobile

Every mockup from the phone design study on one page. The four concepts came first; after critique they converged on a map with a fixed deck, and the decision combined them into "Replay + Photos" (Story in phase 2). The five category sections then design that hybrid in detail, each with its recommended variant and the alternatives.

Phones are 390 × 844. Numbered dots are annotations, explained in the legend under each phone. Photos are gradient stand-ins and the data is the public demo. Wide rows scroll sideways. The decision and requirements are in README.md.

Concept A · Vertical story scroll

A trip is a page you read top to bottom: day chapters, stops and transit breaks, with a passive docked map that holds the current stop and flies only across transits. Touching the map opens Explore (the replay). It reads a trip best and became the phase 2 Story view; its scroll-linked camera is gated by a device spike. Standalone file.

Concept A: Vertical story scroll (v2)

The trip is a page you read. The docked map shows the stop you are reading and only moves when you cross into the next one. One transport bar in the thumb zone (day · ⏮ ▶ ⏭) does every jump and Play. Tapping the map opens Explore, a full-screen map mode with everything at the bottom. Demo data: San Sebastián to Huesca, 6–10 Sep 2024, 194 items.

Not drawn: landscape (map left 50%, story right, each with its own header and transport bar), the day sheet, place search results. v1 keeps the dark basemap in both themes; the light map here is the later target. Red dots are annotations, not UI. Photos are gradient stand-ins; data is the public demo.

Concept B · Horizontal filmstrip

Map on top, a deck in the thumb zone with a docked card and a fixed-playhead filmstrip at two levels (Stops, Photos). It is the cleanest one-handed replay and the closest fit to the engine, but the fixed playhead is an editor idiom that needs a coach. It is the documented runner-up for the strip band. Standalone file.

Concept B v2 · Horizontal filmstrip

One main screen: the map above, a deck in the thumb zone with the docked photo card and a fixed-playhead filmstrip with two levels (Stops, Photos). Picking a trip or a period is a separate Library screen; finding one photo is a grid one swipe up. Revised after the UX and engineering critiques: no ruler, no Years level, no In/Out marks, no tap-to-centre, no peek. Demo data: San Sebastián to Huesca, 6–10 Sep 2024, 194 items.

Concept C · Map-first with a fixed deck

The map is the app; a fixed deck holds the card and a scrubber, and Photos and Library are pushed screens. Its draggable sheet was dropped after critique. Kept from it: the fixed deck with camera insets, place search, "Also here", and year rows with month cells. Standalone file.

Concept C · Map-first with a fixed deck rev. 2

One full-screen map for the whole session. Time lives in a fixed bottom deck (title, cursor card with stop stepping, fixed-playhead strip + Play) that never drags. Photos and the Library are pushed screens; a cluster opens a fixed place sheet that navigates to visits instead of filtering. Demo data: "San Sebastián to Huesca, 6–10 Sep 2024".

What rev. 2 removed

Three-snap draggable sheet, half-snap stop list, the near place filter and its dimmed scrubber, swipe-on-title for prev/next trip, long-press "photos near here", grid scroll moving the cursor.

Landscape

Map left 60%, the deck becomes a right column (title, card, strip stacked); the place sheet, Photos and Library open in that column. Same components, insets.r = column width.

360 × 640

Deck stays 222 px; map keeps ~370 px. The card truncates its place line before it shrinks the 44 px ‹ › targets. Strip ~250 px wide.

Accessibility

Strip is a role="slider" with time, place and "112 of 194" as value text, arrows by photo, Page Up/Down by stop. Map hidden from screen readers; the readout chip and card carry the same text.

States: Trip (deck 222 px), Place sheet (fixed, ~440 px), Photos (pushed, 112 px map lip), Library (pushed). No snap physics. Red dots are annotations, not UI. Photos are gradient stand-ins.

Concept D · Faithful port in tabs

The desktop model through three tabs: Map, Photos, Trips. Cheap and complete, and its Map tab is the layout the Replay view adopted (floating title, Follow/Fit in the thumb arc, docked card, strip). Rejected: the tab bar (83 px to switch between two views) and the Narrow range editor. Standalone file.

Concept D: Faithful port (tabs), revision 2

The desktop model on a phone, cut to three tabs: Map (home), Photos and Trips. Every tab is a lens on one shared selection and time cursor, and links keep the desktop URL contract unchanged. Revision 2 drops the Overview tab: picking a period moves to Trips (year rows, month cells) and to Narrow on the Map tab, so range and route now share one screen.

Navigation · app shell

Stack, no tab bar: Library → Trip (Replay | Photos as views of the trip; Story in phase 2) → Lightbox. Sheets are overlays with their own history entry. Back order = chronology: sheet → lightbox → Library → previous selection; a cold link has nothing behind it, so Back leaves (never trapped, never a fake entry).

Demo data: San Sebastián to Huesca, 6–10 Sep 2024, 194 photos, 6 stops (Sun 8 Sep has no photos and shows as a fold). Chrome follows light/dark; the map is dark in every theme (v1). Pink numbered dots = callouts, listed under each phone. Revised after review 06.
