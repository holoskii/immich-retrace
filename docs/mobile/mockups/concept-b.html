Concept B v2 · Horizontal filmstrip

One main screen: the map above, a deck in the thumb zone with the docked photo card and a fixed-playhead filmstrip with two levels (Stops, Photos). Picking a trip or a period is a separate Library screen; finding one photo is a grid one swipe up. Revised after the UX and engineering critiques: no ruler, no Years level, no In/Out marks, no tap-to-centre, no peek. Demo data: San Sebastián to Huesca, 6–10 Sep 2024, 194 items.

  1. Demo landing (desktop rule: this photo, Follow on, not playing). The film nudges one frame and back once; the coach bubble stays until the first drag. Play pulses once.
  2. Drag the film: slow = live scrub, snaps per photo, camera follows. Fast fling = the film glides, but the cursor (and camera) only moves when it settles on a detent. Writes at with replaceState, never pushState.
  3. Tap any frame = open it in the lightbox (cursor moves to it first). Drag is the only way to seek, so a tap always does one thing.
  4. Pan or pinch the map pauses Follow; the pill turns to "Follow" until tapped. The map never moves time except a pin/cluster tap.
  5. Marker only: time and place moved to the docked card, so there is no floating card on the map.
  6. No-GPS frames have a grey corner (same key as the density band); videos a duration badge.
  7. ▶ Play: deck collapses to film + transport, map grows to ~70%; any touch on the film stops it. Reduced motion: 2.5 s steps, camera jumps per stop.

  1. A pushed screen (not a sheet snap, so no nested scroll): opened by Grid or a swipe up on the deck. ‹, Back or swipe down at the top returns to the map.
  2. Sticky day chapters with the day's stops (from A); stop sub-headers match the film's clips and numbers.
  3. Cuts between stops, same text as the film. No-GPS photos are full members of the grid.
  4. Tap a tile = cursor there + lightbox; closing returns to the map at that photo. Scrolling never moves the cursor (from D), so the map isn't flying behind a screen nobody sees.
  5. Fast-scroll rail with day labels, 16 px from the edge (clear of the OS forward swipe), 44 px hit width.
  6. Back-to-map pill shows the cursor's photo and time. The grid is a DOM list (h2 day, h3 stop, alt text from time + place): the accessible path through the trip.

Gesture ownership: map band = MapLibre (pan, pinch, no rotate). Deck = the film (horizontal: scrub; vertical: up = Grid, down = collapse, decided after 10 px). They never overlap. No double-tap and no long-press anywhere outside Library's month range drag; drags starting within 20 px of either edge belong to the OS.

Lightbox (not drawn, changes from v1): A's day-segmented progress bar at the top replaces "98 / 194"; ‹ › chevrons gone (swipe only); mini film at the bottom uses the same rules as the deck film (drag = seek, tap = show that photo); info sheet with static SVG route; swipe down closes to the map at that photo.

Accessibility: the film is a DOM list in one transformed container with role="slider", aria-valuetext "Mon 9 Sep, 15:12, Santa Cruz de la Serós, photo 98 of 194", arrows = photo, Page Up/Down = stop; map aria-hidden, the docked card is its text equivalent. Landscape: map left 58%, deck becomes a right column (card, film, transport stacked; ~260 px tall).
