Navigation · app shell

Stack, no tab bar: Library → Trip (Replay | Photos as views of the trip; Story in phase 2) → Lightbox. Sheets are overlays with their own history entry. Back order = chronology: sheet → lightbox → Library → previous selection; a cold link has nothing behind it, so Back leaves (never trapped, never a fake entry).

Demo data: San Sebastián to Huesca, 6–10 Sep 2024, 194 photos, 6 stops (Sun 8 Sep has no photos and shows as a fold). Chrome follows light/dark; the map is dark in every theme (v1). Pink numbered dots = callouts, listed under each phone. Revised after review 06.
