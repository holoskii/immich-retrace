Albums · Library, trips, search, ranges

Three variants of the pushed Library screen for Retrace on a phone, plus shared states. Demo data: the six sample albums and the 1 688-photo month manifest of the public demo. Cards marked “illustrative” show an owner library shape (not demo data).

Every result opens one contiguous selection (album, month, month range, custom dates, year, or a visit through zoomInto). No place filter.
