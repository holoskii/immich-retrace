Preview: photo viewing on the phone

Photos/Story layout, lightbox, prefetch. Demo trip "San Sebastián to Huesca", 6–10 Sep 2024, 194 photos (4 without location, 8 short videos in the month). Photos are drawn as gradients; the map is a static SVG sketch. Six stops (Huesca is 44 + 48). Photos is a view of the trip: title block on top, transport row with Replay | Photos at the bottom. Sheets have no grabbers. Pink dots are annotations, not UI. Revised after 06-review.md. Doc: 05-categories/preview.md.

Variant 1 · Story density recommended layout (with V2's rail)

A's justified rows (~108 px from ratio) with a full-width hero per stop. Same component as phase-2 Story; phase 1 shown (scroll never moves the cursor).

Variant 2 · Compact grid

Same component with ratio := 1: 4 square columns (94 px), sticky day + stop headers, no heroes, a fast-scroll rail. Pinch out → Story density; the transport-row toggle does the same without a gesture (after phase 1).

Variant 3 · Lightbox navigation 3a recommended

Shared rules: full-bleed pager, swipe ←/→ by photo (history replace), swipe ↓ closes to the cursor, pinch / double-tap zoom (paging off while zoomed), tap toggles chrome, visible ✕, day-segmented progress, info sheet with a static SVG route. Variants differ in how you see and cross stops: 3a mini filmstrip, 3b none, 3c vertical story pager.
