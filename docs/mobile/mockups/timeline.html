Timeline · time navigation on the phone

Demo trip “San Sebastián to Huesca”, 6–10 Sep 2024: 194 photos, 4 without location, 6 stops. Strip bars use the real distribution, on a folded axis (gaps > 3 h → 14 px breaks). Deck 230 px (V2: 298), map 0 → 614, dark in every theme. Pink dots are annotations, not UI. Design notes: docs/mobile/05-design/timeline.md. Recommended: Variant 3. Revised after the review (06-review.md §3).
